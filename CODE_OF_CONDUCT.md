# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Oscillink Agent contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to a respectful, technically rigorous, and harassment-free environment regardless of experience, background, identity, disability, appearance, nationality, race, ethnicity, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, actionable technical feedback.
- Distinguish observations, hypotheses, vendor claims, and verified results.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licenses, and security boundaries.
- Credit other people's work and preserve provenance.
- Accept correction and update claims when evidence changes.
- Make space for beginners while maintaining the project's testing and safety standards.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Doxxing or publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing credentials, private prompts, customer data, hidden labels, or vulnerability details without authorization.
- Fabricating test results, demonstrations, citations, users, adoption, or safety evidence.
- Pressuring contributors to bypass review, safety, consent, attribution, or licensing requirements.
- Using project spaces primarily for spam, promotion, or unrelated disputes.

## Scope

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, project-operated community spaces, events, and public interactions where someone represents Oscillink Agent.

Technical disagreement, rejection of a contribution, or evidence-based criticism is not harassment when communicated professionally. Safety and product-boundary requirements apply consistently regardless of contributor status.

## Reporting

Do not publish sensitive personal information or detailed security vulnerabilities in a public issue.

When GitHub private vulnerability reporting is available for `Maverick0351a/oscillink-agent`, use it for security matters. For conduct concerns or when a private channel is unavailable, open a minimal issue at <https://github.com/Maverick0351a/oscillink-agent/issues> requesting private maintainer contact without including sensitive details. If the repository is still private and you already have authorized access, contact the repository owner through the existing private collaboration channel.

Reports should include the relevant project space, date, behavior, and available evidence. Share only information necessary to investigate.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other material that violates this code. Responses may include a private warning, temporary participation restriction, removal from project spaces, or permanent exclusion, proportional to severity and pattern of behavior.

Maintainers must protect reporter privacy where practical, avoid retaliation, document enforcement decisions privately, and recuse themselves when they are directly involved. False reports made in bad faith are themselves a violation; an unsupported good-faith report is not.

This policy can be revised as public community channels and maintainership mature. It does not create a guaranteed response time, employment relationship, or legal dispute-resolution process.
